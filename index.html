What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Search is told to skip you.

The homepage code includes a noindex instruction. It tells search engines not to index this page, putting a barrier between CAFÉX and people searching locally. Verified on the live homepage, 22 Oct 2026. Homepage source ↗

02

The menu hides a decision.

The visible site menu shows no prices; Toast does. Guests must open ordering to check cost, adding a step before choosing what to try. Site menu ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

03

The old name still appears.

Toast’s ordering header says CAFÉX, but its location footer still says Con Azucar. That unfinished name change can make a first-time guest pause at the ordering handoff. Toast ordering ↗

04

Social clicks lead to Wix.

The Contact page’s Instagram and Facebook icons point to Wix, not CAFÉX. People trying to follow your story are sent to another brand. The footer’s social links already point to CAFÉX. Contact page ↗

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

05

The homepage stops too soon.

It goes from hero to footer, without video, reviews or story detail. The footer reads ©2035. First-time visitors get little proof before deciding to visit or order. The separate Our Story page works and has substantial detail. Homepage ↗

Shop says “Coming soon” with an email signup, but no launch details. Your planned merch, Mexican roast and podcast need a clearer route from interest to launch. Merch, roast and podcast are your planned launches. Shop page ↗

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.
